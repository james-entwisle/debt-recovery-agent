# Product

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Consumers reviewing a synthetic account need a useful next step without pressure to commit. Internal reviewers need to replay an event, inspect deterministic safety checks, and understand the investment sensitivity.

## Personality and anti-references

Calm, direct and transparent, as requested in the user's execution brief. Avoid chatbot interfaces, decorative AI dashboards and hidden automated commitments.

## Design principles

- Let consumers choose and explain when no approved option fits.
- Show the reason for an action, including taking no action.
- Separate simulated behaviour, supplied evidence and assumptions.
- Use progressive disclosure for policy and financial detail.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Use semantic controls, labelled inputs, visible focus, keyboard navigation, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support. Target WCAG AA contrast as an implementation standard.
