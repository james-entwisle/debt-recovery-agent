# Product Lead case study

**Help consumers take the next useful step**

Decide whether an opportunity deserves investment, build a small demonstration of your recommendation and explain how you would learn from a limited pilot.

## Background

Composer uses AI to draft collections emails and SMS for individual consumers, based on their account, payment history and past engagement. A second model checks each draft for potential compliance issues. Composer is live in Canada, Australia and the UAE, where we test it against templated messages.

Today Composer writes the next message in a fixed contact schedule. It does not respond when someone clicks a message, visits the payment portal, or starts a payment plan and leaves. It is not live in the US, our largest market, where messages are still templated.

We believe a timely, relevant response could help some US consumers make a payment or set up a sustainable plan. We have not decided what the right first investment is.

## Your assignment

You own the first investment decision. Recommend one consumer problem and segment to address, and the smallest useful intervention to test. You may challenge our premise.

You have two engineers, shared access to design and compliance, and four weeks to launch a limited pilot. You can measure longer-term outcomes after launch. The supporting pack sets out the capabilities, economics and pilot constraints.

## Make a recommendation

- Explain the consumer problem, the evidence for it and the assumption most likely to prove you wrong.
- Estimate the value to consumers and to the operator. Use the supplied data, show a few calculations and label your assumptions.
- Choose what to build first, what to leave out and the strongest alternative you considered. If you would defer investment, demonstrate the experiment or diagnostic you would run instead.

## What to bring

### A small working demonstration

Build the core behaviour of your proposal with synthetic data. Code, low-code and spreadsheets all work. Screens alone are not enough unless they show the key decision or interaction.

If you propose a follow-up system, show what happens after an engagement event: whether anything happens, what the consumer sees, when, and how it fits with existing contact activity. For anything else, show the equivalent core behaviour.

- Show a normal case and two meaningful exceptions or failure cases of your choice.
- Make clear what works, what is simulated and what you would build next.
- Include simple instructions so we can run it. A local demonstration is fine; you do not need to deploy it or use paid services.

### A short decision note

In up to two pages, explain your recommendation, commercial reasoning and pilot. Give an engineering team and our compliance lead the right level of guidance to start work, while leaving room for the details they are better placed to decide. Be explicit about which decisions AI makes on its own.

Explain how you would measure the additional value, tell a useful outcome from an encouraging early signal and decide whether to expand, change, pause or stop. Pick a few meaningful metrics and decision criteria; we do not need a detailed specification.

## Time, tools and submission

Spend no more than three hours, including reading time. Stop at the timebox and tell us what is unresolved. We do not need a slide deck. Use AI and any other tools you like; briefly describe what they contributed, and be ready to explain and modify your work. Tool use itself is not scored.

Work from the supplied facts and rules. External research is optional and we do not expect a survey of US law. Everything in the supporting pack is synthetic, so you do not need real consumer data or live messages.

Send your demonstration (or a link to it), run instructions and decision note by the deadline in your invitation. Send questions, or requests to adjust the format or timing, to the contact in your invitation. We share answers to questions with every candidate.

## The discussion

We will spend 60 minutes together: 10 on your recommendation, 15 on the demonstration, 15 on product and commercial decisions, 15 on a new constraint and 5 on your questions. We may ask you to change a small part of your work, or show how you would.

We weight building ability, product judgement and entrepreneurial judgement equally. Consumer safety and honest treatment of evidence are minimum expectations. We value focused, understandable work over breadth or polish.
