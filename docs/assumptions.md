# Assumptions and validation priorities

The [supporting pack](../brief/02_supporting_pack.md) supplies synthetic evidence and exercise economics. The [V2 request](v2-project-brief.txt) proposes conversational resolution. Proposed capabilities are not facts about the current product. The [commercial model](commercial/README.md) retains the original analysis and adds a separate conversational sensitivity.

| Assumption or missing capability | Evidence and risk | Validation before production or expansion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inbound SMS is feasible | The pack establishes outbound contact APIs, not a two-way provider integration, routing or staffing. | Week one, confirm provider reply support, account association, permissions, operational ownership and incremental cost. If unavailable, use a diagnostic or defer. |
| Enough consumers are SMS-reachable | The current scope is the original 18,000, including 10,000 click-only consumers and 8,000 portal visitors. The 60% effective encounter assumption is not measured. The 100,000-person intake is background, not the default target. | Measure current permitted, eligible reach by cohort. Count each person once. Separate availability, delivery and actual exposure. |
| Consumers want to converse with an assistant | Interviews concern portal abandonment; none establishes willingness to reply to AI. A 30% reply rate is illustrative. | User-test disclosure and trust, run approved intent capture, measure replies, abandonment and support outcomes. |
| Conversation creates additional settled cash | No intervention ran. +1/+3/+5 pp among responders are sensitivities, not an effect estimate. Responders select themselves. | Randomise by consumer at t0 and estimate intention-to-treat cash, including non-responders and later suppressed accounts. Report uncertainty. |
| Approved options can meet stated needs | Scenario JSON supplies no real option catalogue. The demo's fixed options and creditor label are separate invented fixtures. | Obtain versioned account-specific approved terms, expiry and availability from the account service. Track unmet preferences without promising changes. |
| A secure task link is possible | Existing links use existing authentication and open a generic landing page. Magic links and purpose-scoped sessions are new proposals. | Security and identity review, shared-device and link-forwarding tests, expiry/reuse/scope tests, recovery and accessibility. Possession of a phone link must not establish identity. |
| Drafts can be saved and resumed | The current portal cannot do this. A plan-start event holds no saved fields. Demo drafts are ephemeral and unconfirmed. | Validate secure persistence, retention, expiry, versioned terms, account rechecks and consumer understanding. Exclude from pilot if infeasible. |
| US message approval is obtainable | US Composer has no production permission, approved prompt set or evaluation. | Human compliance owner approves intents, fixed templates, disclosures and material journey changes. Generated content stays sandbox-only without that pathway. |
| Requested service replies can be safely distinguished | The pack excludes requested service replies from the proactive budget, but gives no complete classification or permission rules. | Human review of specific intent/action pairs, opt-out acknowledgements and help handling. Apply conservative routing and audit; never infer unlimited outbound permission. |
| Escalation can meet consumer needs | Support operates weekday business hours; hardship and dispute need specialist handling. Model intent is not an authoritative hold. | Agree queue ownership, acknowledgement and response targets. Confirm human/account-service hold workflow; test delays and backlog. |
| Persistent context is appropriate | Explicit payday/amount preferences and message text can contain sensitive information. No retention or privacy design is supplied. | Minimise fields, define purpose and retention, restrict access, validate identity and deletion, review vendors and logs. Demo memory disappears on restart or session expiry. |
| Conversation costs remain bounded | The pack's $0.012 is a Composer draft/review attempt, not a validated conversation-turn price. The calculator has editable net SMS, hypothetical AI-turn and other running costs. Zero defaults reproduce the earlier illustration, not production cost certainty. | Measure provider, model/review retries, infrastructure and human oversight costs. Include non-responder costs and maintenance in a real case. |
| Support rises by only two conversations per 100 responders | No incremental support-demand rate is supplied. Avoided support defaults to zero. | Compare conversation counts, effort, backlog and outcomes with control. Credit savings only when measured, not because the assistant answered. |
| Historical cash per payer is a useful marginal proxy | C2/C3/C4 values are $170/$180/$190. An extra plan payment can substitute for another payment rather than create new value. Additional payers may settle less. | Measure cash per assigned consumer directly; sensitivity-test smaller marginal payments. A confirmed plan earns no fee. |
| Equivalent intakes recur for twelve periods | The exercise permits repeated non-overlapping intakes, but actual US scale and durability are unknown. | Verify repeat eligible volume, 60/90-day cash, adherence and treatment decay. Separate acceleration from durable recovery. |
| Replaced sends save their assumed costs | One-for-one replacement can be neutral in the original model; cancellation savings are unverified. | Reconcile actual provider handoffs across channels. Count provider failures as attempts. Cost extra service replies separately. |
| 15% retained fees, 2% cash cost and $6 support apply | Supplied exercise assumptions, not verified company economics. | Confirm client-specific economics before a real investment. |
| $50,000 is an appropriate maximum | Full supplied allocation, not a production quote. New capability scope may exceed it. | Stage spending at the week-one checkpoint. Cost the smaller pilot and strongest alternative; do not force a positive ROI by changing assumptions. |

Unknowns remain unknown. Aggregate cohorts do not provide consumer-level cash variance, abandonment reasons, causal lift or long-term outcomes. The twelve original scenarios are regression examples, not a sample from which to infer rates. The 12-month payback gate is proposed investment policy, distinct from an editable planning target.

Graduation rates are not supplied. The primary calculator uses illustrative current/adaptive path rates calibrated to the earlier +3-point paid-lift case. Effective encounter is a joint exposure assumption; do not apply a second reply filter if already included. The default assumes all additional plan payers are genuinely incremental. Test lower incrementality, smaller payments, no improvement and worse progression. Actual transition and 60/90-day cash evidence must replace these assumptions.
